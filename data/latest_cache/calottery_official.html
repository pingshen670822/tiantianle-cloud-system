How to Play Fantasy 5

Playing is easy. To learn how, follow the 5 simple steps.

STEP 1

Using a Lottery playslip, which you can find at any Lottery retailer, pick 5 numbers between 1 and 39. You can also select Quick Pick®, or just ask the cashier for a Fantasy 5 Quick Pick, and the random number generator will choose the numbers for you.

To play the same numbers for consecutive draws, just mark Advance Play®.

Advance Play allows you to play multiple draws with one purchase.

You can play up to 12 consecutive draws on one playslip by marking the Advance Play box.

STEP 2

Pay $1 per play for each Fantasy 5 ticket. Your ticket is your receipt.

STEP 3

Know the draw times. Fantasy 5 draws take place every day after the draw entry closes at 6:30 p.m.

STEP 4

Remember that Fantasy 5 has 4 winning ball combinations. The more numbers on your ticket that match the numbers drawn, the more you win.

STEP 5

Use any of these ways to see if you’re a Fantasy 5 winner:

  • Look on the Lottery website for winning numbers and prize amounts.
  • Download the Lottery mobile app to see winning numbers.
  • Scan your ticket using the Check-A-Ticket feature on the mobile app.
  • Use the Check-A-Ticket machine at a Lottery retailer location.

How much is the Fantasy 5 top prize?
Each top prize starts between $60,000 to $80,000. If there are no winners, it gets even bigger. If there’s more than one top prize winner, the jackpot is divided equally among them.
When are Fantasy 5 draws held?

Draws take place every day, after the draw entry closes at 6:30 p.m. No sales or ticket validations can take place from 6:30 p.m. to 6:35 p.m., which is when a draw break separates sales during draw periods.

Tickets sold after 6:35 p.m. are ineligible for that night’s draw, but are eligible for the following draw.
How can I find out if I’ve won a draw game?

Use any of these ways to see if you’re a draw game winner:

  • Look on the Lottery website for winning numbers and prize amounts
  • Download the Lottery mobile app to see winning numbers
  • Scan tickets using Check-A-Ticket on the mobile app
  • Use the Check-A-Ticket machine at a Lottery retail location
How do I claim my Fantasy 5 prize?

Collect your prize of up to $599 at any participating Lottery retail location.

Collect your prize over $599 at any Lottery District Office or by mail. Bring your winning ticket and a completed Claim Form to a District Office. Pick up the Claim Form at any Lottery District Office or download the Claim Form (PDF).
How long do I have to claim my Fantasy 5 prize?
You have 180 days from the date of the draw to claim your prize.
Where does the money I spend on Lottery games go?
Ninety-five cents of every player dollar goes back to the community through contributions to public schools and colleges, prizes and retail compensation. See how Lottery funds are making a difference on the Who Benefits page.
Can a prize for a ticket I bought in California be claimed in another state?
No. Your winning ticket must be redeemed in the state where it was purchased.
